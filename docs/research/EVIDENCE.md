## What this means for the product

- "Capture before" holds up: ACS tells people to plan, photograph and match before hair loss.
- The tapped-colour step is a weak match method on its own. ACS's standard is a physical lock of hair compared in daylight. The Match Card should tell people to "cut and bag a lock from the top front" and to treat the colour as a starting point for the fitter, not a final match.
- Do not say "colour ring" is the industry standard. No source supports it.
- The Nao-Art result is a press release, not proof of demand.
